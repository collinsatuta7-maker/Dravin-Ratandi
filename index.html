About Me

A Different Perspective.

Being a mature student is not a limitation. It is a perspective shaped by experience, responsibility, determination and an appreciation for the value of education.

374 KCPE Marks

My name is Ratandi Dravin Nyagechi, a university student at the University of Eldoret whose journey reflects determination, academic capability and a commitment to continuous personal development.

My academic foundation began at Tonga Omonuri Primary School, where I achieved 374 marks in the KCPE. I later continued my secondary education at Moi High School Gesusu, achieving a B+ in the KCSE.

Today, as a mature student at the University of Eldoret, I bring a perspective shaped not only by academic learning but also by maturity and life experience. This journey has strengthened my appreciation for discipline, resilience, responsibility and lifelong learning.

I am focused on developing my knowledge, strengthening my abilities and creating opportunities that allow me to contribute meaningfully to the future.

"The journey may take different paths, but the determination to keep learning creates the destination."
